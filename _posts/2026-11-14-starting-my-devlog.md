This is the first note on my learning log. The plan is simple: **every time I learn something worth remembering about making games, I write it down here.**

## Why write it down?

- Explaining something is the fastest way to find out if I actually understand it.
- Future me will forget the details. Present me can leave notes.
- It's motivating to look back and see the list grow.

## How notes are organized

Every note has one **topic** (Game Design, Programming, Level Design…) and a few **tags**. Each also has a status:

- **seedling**: rough first thoughts
- **budding**: taking shape
- **evergreen**: solid, tested, something I come back to

## Goals for the next few months

- [ ] Finish one small game, start to end
- [ ] Join a game jam
- [ ] Write at least one note a week
